# Accounts

This page explains how to request access to MACC Lab computing resources.

## Who can request access

Access is available to current MACC Lab members including graduate students, undergraduate researchers, and collaborators approved by the PI.

## Required information

When requesting an account, have the following ready:

- Full name
- Stevens Institute email address
- Stevens NetID
- Research group or project affiliation
- Brief description of intended use

## Account approval process

1. Contact the lab system administrator or PI to initiate the request
2. Provide the required information listed above
3. Wait for approval confirmation
4. Receive login credentials and initial setup instructions

## Expected turnaround time

Account requests are typically processed within a few business days. Contact the lab administrator if you have not received a response after one week.

## Contact

Contact your PI or the designated lab system administrator for account requests.
